Cutoff & Payment Schedule

Understand when your work is counted and when your payment may be released. All 26 bi-weekly payroll periods for 2026.

Cutoff period — work counted
Scrutiny — timesheets reviewed
Release week — payment issued
Cutoff Period
The two-week window during which your hours must be submitted. Work completed in this period is counted toward the associated pay period.
Scrutiny Period
The week immediately after the cutoff ends. Timesheets are reviewed and verified by the accounting team during this period.
Release Week
Payment may be issued during this week, subject to client remittance, banking timelines, and operational processing.
