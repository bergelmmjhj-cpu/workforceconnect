1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy is issued by 1001328662 ONTARIO INC., operating the workforce management platform known as Workforce Connect (also referred to as WFConnect). References to "we," "us," "our," or "the Company" in this document refer to 1001328662 ONTARIO INC. and the Workforce Connect platform collectively.

Workforce Connect is operated by 1001328662 ONTARIO INC.

Workforce Connect is a workforce management and staffing platform designed to connect hospitality and janitorial workers with employment opportunities at hotels, commercial facilities, and related venues. Our services include a public application portal at https://apply.wfconnect.org, an administrative platform at https://wfconnect.org, a mobile worker application, and associated communication services including SMS and phone outreach.

We are headquartered in Ontario, Canada, and our operations are primarily governed by Canadian federal and provincial privacy legislation, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Scope of This Policy: This Privacy Policy applies to all individuals who interact with Workforce Connect, including job applicants, registered workers (subcontractors), hotel and facility clients, website visitors, and any other persons who submit personal information to us through any channel.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when it is necessary to operate our staffing and workforce management services. The categories below describe the types of information we collect and the contexts in which we collect them.

2.1 Applicant & Worker Identity Information

  • Full legal name (first, middle, and last)
  • Date of birth
  • Government-issued identification (e.g., driver's licence, passport, provincial ID card)
  • Social Insurance Number (SIN) — collected solely for payroll tax compliance purposes under the Income Tax Act (Canada)
  • Proof of eligibility to work in Canada (e.g., work permit, citizenship documentation)
  • Photograph or scanned image of government-issued ID, where required for identity verification

2.2 Contact Information

  • Mobile and home phone number(s)
  • Email address
  • Home mailing address (street, city, province, postal code)
  • Emergency contact name, relationship, and phone number

2.3 Employment & Application Information

  • Resume / curriculum vitae (CV), including work history and job-related experience
  • Skills, certifications, and training credentials relevant to housekeeping or janitorial roles
  • Work availability and scheduling preferences
  • Employment references
  • Criminal background check results or vulnerable sector checks where mandated by law or client contract
  • Signed subcontractor agreements and consent forms

2.4 Payroll & Financial Information

  • Bank account details for direct deposit (institution number, transit number, account number)
  • Tax filing preferences (TD1 forms)
  • Hours worked, shift records, and time-in / time-out logs
  • Payment history and pay stub records

2.5 Communication Records

  • Text message (SMS) correspondence related to job placement, scheduling, onboarding, and employment updates
  • Phone call logs for interview scheduling and workforce coordination
  • In-app messages through the Workforce Connect mobile application

2.6 Technical & Device Information

When you access our website or mobile application, we automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location (city/region level)
  • Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited, links clicked, session duration, and referral source
  • GPS coordinates — only when you initiate a clock-in or clock-out action (see Section 3)

2.7 Information Collected Through Application Forms

When applicants submit forms through https://apply.wfconnect.org, we collect all fields within those forms, which may include:

  • Preferred work location and shift type
  • Languages spoken
  • Self-identified accommodation needs (if voluntarily disclosed)
  • Consent acknowledgements and digital signatures

3.1 GPS / Location Data (TITO Feature)

When workers use the Time-In / Time-Out feature of the Workforce Connect mobile application, GPS coordinates are captured at the moment of clock-in or clock-out to verify that the worker is at the designated work site. This location data:

  • Is collected only when you actively initiate a clock-in or clock-out action — we do not continuously track your location
  • Is used exclusively to confirm on-site presence for payroll accuracy and client reporting
  • Is retained as part of your employment records and may be used for dispute resolution
  • Will not be used for profiling, marketing, or shared with unrelated third parties

4. SMS & Phone Communication Consent

SMS / 10DLC & A2P Compliance Disclosure

By submitting an application through https://apply.wfconnect.org or otherwise providing your phone number to Workforce Connect (WFConnect) / 1001328662 ONTARIO INC., you expressly consent to receive text messages (SMS) and phone calls from us at the telephone number you provide.

These communications are strictly employment-related and may include:

  • Application status updates and interview scheduling
  • Onboarding instructions and document requests
  • Shift scheduling, assignment confirmations, and schedule changes
  • Shift reminders and last-minute updates
  • Payroll notifications and payment confirmations
  • General employment-related announcements

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of text messages at any time. Reply HELP for assistance. For additional support, contact us at privacy@wfconnect.org or call (437) 476-9566.

For assistance, reply HELP or contact support@wfconnect.org.

4.1 Nature of Consent

Consent to receive SMS and phone communications is a condition of the application process, as these channels are the primary means by which we coordinate interviews, onboarding, and scheduling. You may withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to any text message, by contacting us in writing, or by updating your preferences within the Workforce Connect platform. Please note that withdrawing SMS consent may affect our ability to communicate with you regarding active assignments.

4.2 Messaging Platform

Our SMS and calling services are operated through Zoom Phone and are subject to applicable 10DLC (10-Digit Long Code) and A2P (Application-to-Person) carrier registration requirements. All messages sent through our platform are employment-originated, non-promotional communications registered under our authorized campaign use case.

4.3 Opt-Out Instructions

  • SMS: Reply STOP to any message to immediately stop receiving text messages.
  • Phone Calls: Verbally request removal from calling lists, or submit a written opt-out request to privacy@wfconnect.org.
  • Email: Use the unsubscribe link in any email or send an opt-out request to privacy@wfconnect.org.

5. We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information

Important Privacy Commitment

We do not sell, rent, share, or distribute personal information, including phone numbers, to third parties for marketing, lead generation, or promotional purposes.

This commitment applies fully and without exception to all categories of personal information we collect, including but not limited to:

  • Names and contact information (phone numbers, email addresses, mailing addresses)
  • Social Insurance Numbers (SIN) and government-issued identification
  • Payroll and financial data
  • Employment history, resumes, and credentials
  • Location and attendance data
  • Any other personal information collected through our application forms, mobile app, or website

We do not participate in data broker relationships, data monetization programs, or any arrangement in which personal information is exchanged for consideration. Your data is used exclusively to provide, operate, and improve Workforce Connect's staffing and workforce management services for employment purposes.

Clarification for Compliance Reviewers

The above non-sale commitment is absolute. No carve-out exists for marketing, advertising networks, lead generation companies, or data aggregators. Phone numbers collected through SMS consent flows are used solely for operational employment communications described in Section 4 of this policy.

SMS Communications & Personal Information

Workforce Connect does not sell, rent, share, or disclose consumer personal information, including phone numbers, to third parties or affiliates for marketing, promotional, or lead generation purposes.

Phone numbers collected for SMS consent are used only for Workforce Connect service-related communications, including job opportunities, shift updates, scheduling, reminders, and support.

Users may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP or request assistance by replying HELP. Message and data rates may apply.

6. Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not disclose your personal information to third parties except in the limited circumstances described below. All third parties who receive personal information from us are bound by contractual confidentiality obligations and are permitted to use the data only for the specified purpose.

6.1 Client Hotels and Facilities

When you are placed on an assignment at a client hotel, resort, or facility, we share operationally necessary information with that client, which may include your name, contact information, certifications, and attendance records. This disclosure is necessary to facilitate your work engagement and is covered under your subcontractor agreement.

6.2 Payroll & Financial Service Providers

We work with payroll service providers and banking institutions to process payments and issue pay stubs. These providers receive the minimum necessary financial data to complete transactions on your behalf.

6.3 Technology & Infrastructure Providers

We use reputable cloud hosting, database management, and communication technology providers to operate our platform. These providers process data on our behalf as data processors and are contractually prohibited from using your data for any purpose other than providing services to us.

6.4 Legal and Regulatory Requirements

We may disclose personal information when required by applicable law, court order, search warrant, or government authority, or when necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations under Canadian federal or provincial law
  • Respond to lawful requests from law enforcement or regulatory bodies
  • Protect the legal rights, property, or safety of Workforce Connect, our workers, clients, or the public
  • Investigate fraud, security incidents, or policy violations

6.5 Business Continuity

In the event of a merger, acquisition, corporate restructuring, or sale of assets involving 1001328662 ONTARIO INC., personal information may be transferred to a successor entity. Any such transfer will be subject to equivalent privacy protections and, where required by law, individual notice.

6.6 Aggregated and Anonymized Data

We may use aggregated, de-identified, or anonymized data (from which individual identities have been irreversibly removed) for operational reporting and service improvement. Such data cannot reasonably identify any individual and is not considered personal information under PIPEDA.

7. Data Security

We implement a layered set of technical, administrative, and physical safeguards proportionate to the sensitivity of the data we hold.

7.1 Technical Measures

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 / 1.3.
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive records including government ID data, SIN, and financial details are encrypted at rest using AES-256 or equivalent.
  • Password Security: User account passwords are hashed using bcrypt or equivalent one-way algorithms and are never stored in plaintext.
  • Access Controls: Role-based access controls (RBAC) ensure staff can only access personal information relevant to their specific function.
  • Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Administrative portal access is protected by multi-factor authentication.
  • Regular Security Updates: Our systems undergo regular security assessments, dependency updates, and vulnerability patching.

7.2 Administrative Measures

  • Confidentiality obligations for all staff and contractors with access to personal data
  • Internal privacy training for staff handling sensitive applicant information
  • Documented data handling procedures and incident response protocols

7.3 Limitations and Breach Notification

While we employ industry-standard measures, no system is perfectly secure. In the event of a confirmed data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as required under PIPEDA's mandatory breach reporting rules.

10. Your Rights & Opt-Out

As a Canadian resident, you have rights under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation.

10.1 Right of Access

You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and to receive information about how it is being used and disclosed. We will respond to verified access requests within 30 days, as required by PIPEDA.

10.2 Right to Correction

If personal information we hold about you is inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated, you may request a correction. We will update records promptly and, where appropriate, notify any third parties to whom we have disclosed the information.

10.3 Right to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw consent for the collection, use, or disclosure of your personal information at any time, subject to legal and contractual restrictions. Withdrawal of consent for non-mandatory purposes will not affect employment decisions.

10.4 Right to Deletion

Subject to applicable legal hold requirements, you may request deletion of your personal information. Where records must be retained under statutory obligations, we will inform you of the applicable retention period.

10.5 Right to Opt Out of SMS / Phone / Email Communications

  • Reply STOP to any SMS message to immediately unsubscribe from text messages
  • Reply HELP to any SMS message to receive contact support information
  • Email privacy@wfconnect.org to opt out of phone or email communications
  • Submit a written opt-out request through the Workforce Connect worker portal

10.6 Right to Lodge a Complaint

If your concern has not been resolved through our internal process, you may file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC):

  • Website: www.priv.gc.ca
  • Toll-free: 1-800-282-1376

10.7 Account Deletion Request

Workers may request full account deletion by navigating to account settings within the Workforce Connect app or by contacting privacy@wfconnect.org. Payroll and tax records may be retained as required by law regardless of account deletion.

11. Children's Privacy

The Workforce Connect platform and application portal are intended exclusively for individuals who are legally eligible for employment in Canada — a minimum of 18 years of age (or the applicable provincial minimum working age, whichever is higher).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a minor, we will promptly delete that information. If you believe a minor has submitted information through our platform, please contact us immediately at privacy@wfconnect.org.

12. Legal Basis for Processing (PIPEDA & Canadian Privacy Law)

Workforce Connect's collection, use, and disclosure of personal information is governed by the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), S.C. 2000, c. 5, as well as applicable Ontario and provincial employment and privacy legislation.

We collect and use personal information on the following bases:

  • Consent: You have given express consent (e.g., by completing an application form, signing a subcontractor agreement, or providing your phone number for SMS communications)
  • Contractual Necessity: Processing is necessary to perform our obligations under your subcontractor agreement
  • Legal Obligation: Processing is required to comply with applicable Canadian law (e.g., tax reporting, employment records under the Employment Standards Act)
  • Legitimate Interest: Processing is in our legitimate interest to operate a staffing platform, prevent fraud, and maintain platform security — where those interests are not overridden by your privacy interests

Cross-Border Note: Our servers are located in Canada and/or the United States. If you are located outside Canada, by submitting information to us you acknowledge that your data may be transferred to and processed in Canada or the United States, which may have different privacy laws than your country of residence.
